# Adams Erben — Hero Skiff Scene Prompt

Use the complete [`master-visual-prompt.md`](./master-visual-prompt.md) before this scene specification.

## Scene

**YEAR / PERIOD:**  
approximately 1959–1960

**LOCATION:**  
Ratzeburger See, Ratzeburg, Schleswig-Holstein, Northern Germany

**SUBJECT:**  
one young German male competitive sculler, approximately 22–30 years old

He has the believable physique of a high-level rower of the period: lean, strong back, developed shoulders and thighs, but not muscular like a modern fitness model.

Dark natural hair, slightly wet and untidy from training.

He wears a simple off-white cotton rowing singlet and dark navy short athletic shorts appropriate to the late 1950s.

No logos and no modern sportswear.

**ACTIVITY:**  
The athlete is alone in a historically plausible wooden racing single scull during a serious training session.

He is not posing.

His attention is entirely directed toward rowing.

He does not look toward the camera.

Show visible but restrained exertion: slightly flushed skin, perspiration and concentration.

**ROWING DISCIPLINE:**  
single scull / 1x

Exactly one rower.

Exactly two sculls.

One handle firmly and naturally held in each hand.

Both shafts must pass correctly through their respective oarlocks.

**STROKE PHASE:**  
early-to-mid recovery, technically coherent

The hands, arms, torso, sliding seat and blades must all correspond to the same phase.

The athlete should be moving naturally toward the catch.

Do not create a dramatic or exaggerated rowing pose.

**BOAT:**  
a technically credible late-1950s wooden racing single scull

Long, narrow wooden hull.

Period-appropriate sliding seat, foot stretcher, metal riggers and oarlocks.

Wooden sculls with historically plausible blades.

Visible subtle wear and varnished wooden texture.

Absolutely no modern carbon fibre.

**CAMERA POSITION:**  
camera approximately 40–70 cm above the water surface, positioned several metres to the side of the racing shell as if photographed from another low boat travelling alongside

The camera position must be physically possible.

Approximately 50–85 mm equivalent photographic perspective.

Avoid ultra-wide-angle distortion.

**FRAMING:**  
very wide horizontal cinematic panorama

The entire relationship between athlete, shell, sculls and water must remain readable.

Do not crop hands, oarlocks or important rowing geometry.

Place the rower somewhat right of centre.

Preserve substantial quiet negative space toward the left side of the image for website headline typography.

**ASPECT RATIO:**  
3:1 preferred

Suitable for a full-width website hero.

**LIGHT:**  
clear to lightly hazy Northern German afternoon

Low-to-medium angle sunlight.

Warm skin tones may be present, but the scene must not resemble sunset photography.

No spectacular golden-hour glow.

No exaggerated rim light.

**ENVIRONMENT:**  
Ratzeburger See with calm but naturally textured water.

Low wooded shoreline.

Mixed deciduous vegetation.

Very subtle distant indication of Ratzeburg architecture may be visible, but the background must remain secondary.

No cliffs.

No mountains.

No Mediterranean vegetation.

No dramatic coastline.

**WATER:**  
Natural small ripples and minor wake from the shell.

Sculls may produce small irregular droplets or surface disturbance.

Reflections should be imperfect and physically credible.

Avoid symmetrical decorative splashes.

**MOOD:**  
quiet concentration and disciplined training

The image should feel as though a cinematographer captured one excellent frame during an actual training outing.

It should not feel staged for a poster.

## Particularly important

The scene must appear photographed rather than generated.

The athlete should look like a real German competitive rower from around 1960 rather than a contemporary actor or fitness model.

Prioritize:

- authenticity over beauty
- observation over drama
- physical credibility over spectacular composition
- rowing accuracy over visual effect

## Do not include

- bodybuilding physique
- fashion-model styling
- Mediterranean scenery
- sunset spectacle
- heroic gaze
- looking at camera
- modern rowing boat
- carbon fibre
- modern sportswear
- mirrored sunglasses
- modern watch
- perfect symmetrical composition
- artificial cinematic glow
- orange-and-teal grade
- HDR
- CGI appearance
- concept-art appearance
- implausible hands
- disconnected oar handles
- bent or malformed shafts
- incorrect oarlocks
- duplicate equipment
- impossible water physics

The final result should resemble an authentic production still from a late-1950s German rowing story photographed today with exceptional historical authenticity.
