# Adams Erben — Rowing boat schemata prompts

Stand: 2026-08-16  
Branch: `image-production`

## Purpose

These prompts replace the current very simple CSS/SVG-like schemata in the `Rudern verstehen` comparison cards with two visually related, technically credible rowing illustrations:

- `Einer · 1x` — single scull
- `Achter · 8+` — coxed eight

The two images must look as if they were designed as one illustration family. They are **not photographic hero imagery** and therefore should not be generated by blindly applying the photographic `master-visual-prompt.md` aesthetic. Instead, use the shared diagram style lock below for both assets.

The current website cards use different backgrounds. The illustration language must stay identical; only the palette may be contrast-adapted for the light and dark card.

---

## Shared diagram style lock — apply verbatim to both prompts

Create a refined editorial rowing diagram for a premium cultural and sports website.

Use a strict near-orthographic top-down view with the complete racing shell visible from bow to stern. The drawing should feel like a carefully art-directed technical sports illustration: more tactile and dimensional than a flat SVG icon, but much more restrained than a photorealistic render.

### Visual language

- clean, calm, precise editorial illustration;
- direct top-down / near-orthographic geometry;
- no dramatic perspective and no foreshortening;
- long horizontal composition;
- subtle material definition in hull, riggers, seats and oar shafts;
- very restrained soft shading only where it helps distinguish overlapping components;
- matte surfaces, no glossy 3D-render look;
- crisp edges without heavy outlines;
- no watercolor, no comic style, no blueprint style, no infographic clip-art look;
- no background scenery, no water texture, no wake and no landscape;
- transparent background preferred;
- no drop shadow around the complete illustration;
- no decorative elements unrelated to rowing geometry.

### Adams Erben palette

Use only the project palette unless a neutral material tone is required:

- navy `#0B2336`;
- secondary navy `#123A57`;
- light blue `#92D6EA`;
- secondary light blue `#A9DCEB`;
- accent red `#A6342B`;
- foam `#F7F5EF`;
- white `#FFFFFF`.

The two assets must use the same line weight, same degree of shading, same hull material treatment, same oar treatment and same visual scale.

### Technical priorities

Rowing accuracy has priority over visual elegance.

- preserve physically possible hull proportions;
- show plausible sliding-seat and rigger geometry;
- oar/scull shafts must pass through plausible oarlocks;
- shafts must not intersect the hull, seats or crew incorrectly;
- no extra oars, missing oars, duplicate riggers or impossible crossings;
- every handle must correspond to exactly one blade;
- all equipment must be mechanically connected and visually traceable.

### Typography rule

Do **not** generate words, numerals or labels inside the image. Generative typography is not part of the asset. Seat numbers, `BUG`, `HECK`, `Steuer` or similar labels should be overlaid later in HTML/CSS if required.

### Anti-AI constraints

Avoid malformed blades, doubled shafts, merged handles, asymmetric duplicate riggers, impossible hull openings, floating equipment, invented seats, non-rowing paddles, decorative fins, perspective drift, inconsistent left/right scale, text fragments, pseudo-letters and stray marks.

# Prompt A — `Einer · 1x`

## Target asset

Suggested asset family: `rowing-schema-1x`  
Preferred production master: transparent PNG or WebP, at least `1600 × 650 px`, approximately `2.4:1` to `2.7:1`.

## Prompt

Apply the complete **Shared diagram style lock** above.

Create a technically correct top-down editorial illustration of a modern racing single scull (`1x`).

The complete boat is horizontal, with **bow on the left and stern on the right**. Show exactly **one rower**, exactly **one sliding seat**, exactly **two sculls**, and exactly **two riggers/oarlocks**, one on each side.

The hull must be long, extremely narrow and symmetrical around its longitudinal axis, with a fine pointed bow and narrow stern. The rower sits on the centerline. Show enough of the cockpit geometry to make the sliding seat, foot stretcher area and the two outriggers believable without turning the image into a dense engineering drawing.

The rower is seen directly from above in a neutral mid-stroke / neutral demonstration position. Both arms and hands must be mechanically plausible: **one hand holds the port scull handle and the other hand holds the starboard scull handle**. The two shafts leave the boat through separate oarlocks and extend to opposite sides. The two sculls may use the same blade orientation and must have matching proportions.

Keep the sculls visually balanced and clearly separated. No crossed shafts, no third oar, no paddle geometry.

### Contrast-adapted palette for the light comparison card

The asset will sit on a very light neutral card. Use:

- hull primarily navy `#0B2336`, with very restrained secondary-navy material shading;
- riggers and technical details in navy / muted light blue;
- scull shafts predominantly light blue `#92D6EA`;
- blade faces may use accent red `#A6342B` as the strongest color accent;
- rower/seat details in navy, foam and subtle light blue.

The red should behave as a controlled Adams Erben accent, not as a large glossy surface.

### Composition

Center the boat horizontally and vertically with generous transparent margin around both blades. Keep the visual mass compact enough to work above the `Einer · 1x` heading in a card. The illustration must remain readable at roughly 450–550 px displayed width.

Do not include any numbers, captions, arrows, legends, water, shoreline or text.

## Mandatory QA for the 1x result

Reject the image unless all are true:

1. exactly one rower;
2. exactly two sculls;
3. exactly one blade per shaft;
4. exactly one scull on each side;
5. both hands plausibly connect to the two handles;
6. both shafts pass through plausible oarlocks;
7. no shaft intersects the hull or body impossibly;
8. complete hull and both complete blades are visible;
9. bow is left, stern is right;
10. no generated text or stray symbols.

# Prompt B — `Achter · 8+`

## Target asset

Suggested asset family: `rowing-schema-8plus`  
Preferred production master: transparent PNG or WebP, at least `2200 × 650 px`, approximately `3.4:1` to `4:1`.

## Prompt

Apply the complete **Shared diagram style lock** above.

Create a technically correct top-down editorial illustration of a modern coxed racing eight (`8+`).

The complete racing shell is horizontal, with **bow on the left and stern on the right**. Show exactly **eight rowers plus one coxswain**. All eight rowing seats lie on one longitudinal centerline. Seat 1 is nearest the bow; seat 8 / stroke is nearest the stern; the coxswain is in the classic stern position immediately behind seat 8 at the far right end of the crew area.

Show exactly **eight sweep oars total** — one and only one oar per rower. Show exactly **four port and four starboard oars**. The rowing sides must alternate strictly from seat to seat along the hull. Adjacent rowers may never have their oars on the same side.

Each shaft must be traceable from one rower's hands through exactly one oarlock to exactly one blade. Riggers and oarlocks must align with the corresponding seat. No floating oars and no crossed or merged shafts. The alternating pattern must remain visually obvious even at card size.

All rowers are shown from above in the same neutral demonstration phase. Their posture and equipment geometry should be consistent. The coxswain must be visually distinct as a non-rowing ninth person and must not have an oar.

Do not render seat numbers in the image; the website may overlay `1–8` separately in HTML/CSS if desired.

### Contrast-adapted palette for the dark comparison card

The asset will sit on a dark navy card (`#143349` / `#0B2336`). Use:

- hull primarily light blue `#92D6EA` with restrained `#A9DCEB` variation;
- oar shafts in light blue / foam;
- blades in light blue, foam or very restrained accent-red details if needed;
- seats, rowers and technical details in foam `#F7F5EF`, white and light blue;
- use navy only for internal separation where sufficient contrast remains.

Do not turn the illustration into a glowing cyan neon graphic. Surfaces remain matte and editorial.

### Composition

Use a long, low horizontal composition with generous transparent margin around all eight blades and the full hull. Keep the hull centered, with no perspective taper caused by the camera. The result must remain legible at roughly 450–550 px displayed width, so simplify fine detail while keeping crew count and side alternation unmistakable.

Do not include any numbers, captions, arrows, legends, water, shoreline or text.

## Mandatory QA for the 8+ result

Reject the image unless all are true:

1. exactly eight rowers;
2. exactly one coxswain;
3. exactly nine people total;
4. exactly eight rowing seats;
5. exactly eight sweep oars;
6. exactly four oars on each side;
7. rowing sides alternate strictly seat by seat;
8. each rower has exactly one oar;
9. the coxswain has no oar;
10. seat sequence is spatially correct: seat 1 at bow, seat 8 at stern, coxswain behind seat 8;
11. every shaft connects through a plausible oarlock;
12. no duplicated or merged riggers, shafts or blades;
13. complete hull and all eight complete blades are visible;
14. bow is left, stern is right;
15. no generated text or stray symbols.

## Pair consistency gate

Do not approve either asset in isolation. Review the `1x` and `8+` side by side and reject the pair if any of the following differs noticeably:

- camera angle / top-down geometry;
- edge sharpness;
- line weight;
- shading strength;
- hull material language;
- oar material language;
- blade shape family;
- rigger simplification level;
- amount of empty space around the boat;
- overall visual density.

The two assets should read immediately as **the same illustration system applied to two different boat classes**.
