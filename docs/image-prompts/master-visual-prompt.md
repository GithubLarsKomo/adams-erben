# Adams Erben — Master Visual Prompt

Create a highly authentic photographic image from a late-1950s to early-1960s German rowing environment.

The image must feel like a genuine frame from a carefully photographed European historical sports drama rather than a modern commercial photograph or an AI-generated cinematic illustration.

## Visual language

Use restrained, observational cinematography with strong documentary realism.

The camera should appear to be witnessing a real event rather than staging a heroic scene.

The visual character should evoke:

- late-1950s / early-1960s German sports photography
- European historical drama cinematography
- tactile documentary realism
- natural human imperfection
- physically authentic rowing
- subtle period atmosphere
- restrained cinematic composition
- understated emotional intensity

The result should look like an actual photographed moment from a high-quality historical film production.

Do not make the image picturesque merely for aesthetic effect.

## Photographic character

Simulate a real 35 mm motion-picture frame photographed with period-inspired optics.

Use:

- natural available daylight whenever possible
- soft but imperfect exposure
- moderate dynamic range
- restrained contrast
- subtle film grain
- fine photographic texture
- slightly muted colors
- natural skin tones
- gentle highlight roll-off
- slightly softened optical rendering
- realistic depth of field
- subtle atmospheric haze where appropriate
- occasional minor optical imperfections
- natural motion where physically appropriate

Avoid excessive sharpness.

Avoid the hyper-detailed appearance of modern digital commercial photography.

The image must retain texture in skin, fabric, wood, water and vegetation.

## Color palette

Use a restrained Central European period palette:

- warm but natural skin tones
- off-white and cream cotton clothing
- dark navy
- muted black
- weathered brown wood
- subdued forest green
- cool lake blue
- grey-blue sky
- restrained turquoise where historically or architecturally appropriate
- occasional period red, yellow or blue accents

Colors should feel slightly aged but not artificially sepia-toned.

Never use an exaggerated orange-and-teal movie grade.

## Light

Light must feel geographically plausible for Northern Germany.

Prefer:

- clear or lightly hazy daylight
- low or medium-angle sunlight
- overcast northern European light
- filtered window light
- soft late-afternoon illumination

Sunset or golden-hour lighting may only be used when the scene specifically requires it.

Even then, keep it natural and restrained.

Avoid:

- spectacular rim lighting
- artificial glow around people
- strong lens flare
- HDR lighting
- dramatic Hollywood backlighting
- unrealistic volumetric light
- glossy advertising illumination

## People

People must look like real individuals rather than actors posing as athletes or fitness models.

They should have:

- believable period hairstyles
- natural facial asymmetry
- realistic skin texture
- visible pores where appropriate
- perspiration during exercise
- slightly disordered hair during training
- believable physical fatigue
- natural posture
- historically plausible body composition

Rowers should look athletic because they row, not because they are bodybuilders.

Muscular development should primarily reflect rowing:

- strong back
- shoulders
- forearms
- thighs
- relatively lean body composition

Avoid:

- bodybuilding physiques
- exaggerated abdominal definition
- fashion-model proportions
- cosmetic skin
- perfectly symmetrical faces
- heroic facial expressions
- direct camera engagement unless specifically requested

## Performance

Whenever people are performing an activity, show them engaged in that activity rather than posing.

Expressions should emerge naturally from:

- concentration
- fatigue
- effort
- communication
- observation
- anticipation
- recovery

Small imperfections make the scene credible.

Clothing may be wrinkled.

Hair may be wet or untidy.

Skin may be flushed.

The subject does not need to occupy the visual center.

## Rowing authenticity — critical

All rowing equipment and biomechanics must be technically credible.

Prioritize rowing accuracy over visual drama.

For rowing scenes:

- use historically plausible racing shells
- use historically plausible riggers
- correctly constructed oarlocks
- realistic wooden oars or sculls where appropriate
- correct blade geometry for the period
- correct handle placement
- physically possible hand positions
- correct seating position
- believable sliding-seat geometry
- correct foot stretcher placement
- correct relationship between rower, boat and oars
- realistic hull proportions
- realistic freeboard
- believable water displacement

Every athlete must actually hold the correct handle or handles.

Hands must mechanically connect to the oars.

Oars must physically connect through the oarlocks.

Oar shafts must not intersect bodies, riggers or the hull impossibly.

For sculling:

- exactly two sculls per rower
- both hands correctly holding one handle each

For sweep rowing:

- exactly one sweep oar per rower

When showing multiple rowers, verify crew count, seat sequence and side arrangement.

Do not invent ornamental or fantasy rowing equipment.

## Rowing motion

Show believable phases of the rowing stroke.

The body, seat, arms and blades must correspond to the same stroke phase.

Water interaction must be physically believable.

Allow:

- irregular droplets
- small wake disturbances
- asymmetric splashes
- wet boat surfaces
- imperfect water reflections

Avoid decorative symmetrical splashes.

## Historical environment

Everything visible should be plausible for approximately 1955–1965 unless another period is explicitly requested.

Check:

- clothing
- footwear
- watches
- architecture
- boats
- cars
- street furniture
- typography
- signage
- hairstyles
- training equipment
- materials

No contemporary objects should appear.

Avoid modern carbon-fibre rowing shells, modern Concept2-style oar handles, current sportswear, contemporary sunglasses or modern architecture unless specifically requested.

## Geography

When set in Ratzeburg, Northern Germany, or Schleswig-Holstein, depict the geography accurately.

Typical environmental characteristics:

- calm or lightly disturbed inland lake
- low wooded shoreline
- deciduous and mixed woodland
- restrained northern European vegetation
- low rolling terrain
- modest historic town architecture
- northern German atmospheric conditions

Do not depict:

- Mediterranean cliffs
- Californian landscapes
- Alpine mountains
- Italian lakes
- dramatic rocky coastlines
- tropical vegetation

When geographically useful, distant Ratzeburg landmarks may appear subtly and naturally.

Never turn them into postcard landmarks unless explicitly requested.

## Composition

Use observational film composition rather than advertising composition.

Good characteristics include:

- off-center subjects
- partial foreground obstruction
- imperfect framing
- negative space
- subjects entering or leaving the frame
- natural environmental layering
- subtle visual imbalance
- cropped equipment where compositionally plausible
- camera positions dictated by the physical environment

Avoid:

- perfectly centered heroes
- symmetrical poster compositions
- heroic low-angle advertising shots
- artificially clean backgrounds
- visual arrangements that appear designed for stock photography

## Camera position

Choose physically plausible camera locations.

Examples:

- close to water level from another launch or boat
- from the pontoon
- from the shoreline
- from inside the boathouse
- beside the training area
- documentary shoulder-height observation

Do not use impossible floating camera perspectives.

## Texture

Preserve tactile material qualities:

Wood should show grain, wear and varnish variation.

Cotton should visibly behave like cotton.

Metal should have realistic wear rather than perfect chrome.

Painted surfaces may show age.

Water should remain complex and irregular.

Skin should retain human texture.

## Emotional tone

The image should communicate:

- discipline
- concentration
- precision
- shared effort
- quiet ambition
- technical curiosity
- physical work
- the seriousness of competitive rowing

Avoid sentimental nostalgia.

Avoid exaggerated triumph.

Avoid superhero imagery.

The emotional register should remain restrained.

## Anti-AI constraints

The image must not display common generative-image characteristics.

Specifically avoid:

- glossy AI skin
- hyper-perfect faces
- excessive cinematic glow
- orange-and-teal grading
- exaggerated sunset
- fantasy geography
- impossible anatomy
- extra fingers
- fused fingers
- duplicated objects
- malformed oars
- floating equipment
- impossible riggers
- geometrically impossible boats
- decorative water splashes
- excessive bokeh
- unnaturally perfect reflections
- excessive micro-detail
- HDR appearance
- concept-art rendering
- digital painting appearance
- fashion photography
- sports advertising
- luxury-brand imagery

The final image should be easy to mistake for a professionally restored production still from a historical European film.

# Final quality instruction

Before rendering, internally verify:

1. historical plausibility,
2. rowing mechanics,
3. human anatomy,
4. equipment geometry,
5. geographic plausibility,
6. lighting plausibility,
7. absence of modern objects,
8. absence of stereotypical AI aesthetics.

If cinematic beauty conflicts with historical or technical credibility, always choose credibility.
